1 Summary

We take your privacy seriously. The App does not require any sign-up, login, or account creation. Your game progress, settings, and preferences are stored entirely on your own device, and we do not collect or transmit this data to any server we operate. The App displays advertisements through a third party, Google AdMob, which may collect certain device-related information as explained in Section 3.

2 Information We Collect

2.1 What We Do NOT Collect

The App does not collect, and does not request permission for, any of the following:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information
  • Location / GPS data
  • Camera or photo/gallery access
  • Microphone or audio recording
  • Contacts list or data from any other app on your device
  • Any form of sign-up, login, or social media account linking

2.2 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

For the App to function correctly, the following information is stored only in local storage on your device (an SQLite database and Android SharedPreferences). This data is never transmitted to us or to any third-party server:

  • Game progress, including which levels you have reached and completed
  • Generated puzzle level data (grid size, arrow path configuration)
  • Your preferences, including selected theme, selected playable style, and sound, music, and vibration settings
Note: This data is permanently deleted from your device when you uninstall the App.

2.3 Automatically Collected Technical Information

If the App crashes or encounters an error, the Android operating system may collect basic diagnostic information (device model, Android version, app version) for troubleshooting purposes. This information does not identify you personally.

3 Advertising — Google AdMob

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob, a service operated by Google. In order to serve, measure, and optimize ads, AdMob may automatically collect the following information:

  • Advertising ID: Google Advertising ID (GAID). It is not personally identifiable, and you can reset or limit it in your device settings
  • Device information: model, operating system, and screen size
  • Approximate location: country or region derived from your IP address (we and AdMob do not collect precise GPS data)
  • Ad events: ad requests, impressions, clicks, bidding, and attribution
  • App performance and diagnostic data: such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage, collected by the AdMob SDK for analytics purposes

We share this technical data with Google AdMob only for the purpose of displaying ads, measuring ad performance, and helping ensure that ads are relevant to you where permitted by law.

Your choices

  • For users in the EEA, UK, and certain U.S. states, we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to collect and manage your consent choices regarding ad personalization before any ad request is made. You can review or change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy Options.
  • You can control ad personalization at any time from your device's Google Settings → Ads by enabling "Opt out of Ads Personalization." You can also reset your Advertising ID in your device settings.

How AdMob uses this data is governed by Google's own policies

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4 Vibration and Audio Permissions

The App uses the Vibration permission to provide haptic feedback (for example, a light vibration when you make a mistake), and uses your device's audio output to play sound effects and background music. Both operate entirely on your device, do not collect or transmit any data, and can be turned off at any time from the in-app Settings.

5 Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Because we do not collect personal information ourselves, there is essentially nothing for us to share. The only exceptions are the technical data exchange with our advertising partner, Google AdMob, for the purpose of displaying ads (described in Section 3), and cases where disclosure is required by law or a valid governmental request.

6 Children's Privacy

The App is intended for an adult audience (18 years of age and older) and is not directed at children or minors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or minors. In fact, we do not directly collect personal information from any user.

Google Play may restrict users whom it determines to be under 18 from searching for or downloading the App when the applicable age restriction setting is enabled.

If you are a parent or guardian and have any concerns about the App or its privacy practices, please contact us at liorxsupport@gmail.com.

7 Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we do not store personal data on any server, most data-related rights (access, correction, deletion) are already in your control. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all locally stored data. For rights related to advertising data (such as your Advertising ID), you may use Google's privacy tools at https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy, or contact us directly.

8 Data Security

Your game progress and settings are stored only on your own device, within Android's app-sandboxing security model, so this data is protected by the operating system itself. Because we do not maintain a central server holding your data, the risk of a data breach affecting your information is minimized.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page along with an updated "Last Updated" date above.
